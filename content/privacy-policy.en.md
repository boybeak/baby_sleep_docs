# Privacy Policy

> App: Baby Sleep
> Version: v1.0
> Effective date: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **individual developer [Developer Name]** ("we", the developer and data controller of this App) handles information when you use Baby Sleep (the "App"). **Our design principle is to handle as little information as possible: the App requires no registration or sign-in, and we do not operate our own servers to store your personal data.**

## 1. Overview

1. We do **not collect or upload** your personally identifiable information.
2. The App contains no advertising, and does not integrate any third-party advertising or behavioural analytics/tracking SDK.
3. Information required for the App to run is stored **only on your device** and is never sent back to us.

## 2. Information We Handle

### 2.1 Information stored locally on your device

To provide its basic features, the App stores necessary configuration locally on your device, for example:

| Information | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement acceptance status (whether you have agreed to the User Agreement and this Privacy Policy) | To decide whether to show the first-launch agreement dialog | Local app data on your device |

This information does not contain your identity information, and we cannot read it remotely.

> Note: If features such as "remembering the last played sound or timer setting" are added later, such preferences will likewise be stored only locally, and this Policy will be updated accordingly.

### 2.2 Information we do not collect

We do not collect or upload: your name, phone number, email address, account, contacts, precise location, photo/file contents, microphone or camera contents, or advertising identifiers used for cross-app tracking.

### 2.3 Permissions the App requests and their purposes

| Permission / capability | Purpose | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications (Android 13+: POST_NOTIFICATIONS) | To show playback controls and the current sound in the notification shade and on the lock screen | Needed for background playback; declining does not affect foreground playback |
| Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK) | To keep sound playing when the screen is locked or the app is in the background, so playback is not interrupted by the system | Needed for background playback |
| Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK) | To keep audio running properly during playback | Needed for background playback |

These permissions are used solely to provide sound playback features, not to collect information.

### 2.4 Third-party components

1. **App stores and payments**: The App is distributed through Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). When you download the App or purchase a subscription, **payments and transactions are handled by the respective platform**; we do not have access to your payment card information. The platforms may process related data (such as transaction records and diagnostics) under their own privacy policies. We do not control such processing; please refer to Google's and Apple's privacy policies.
2. **App integrity / security checks (if enabled)**: If security capabilities such as app integrity checks are enabled in the future to prevent tampering and fraud, such checks may be processed by the platform (for example, Google) under its policies. We will update this Policy before enabling them.
3. Apart from the above and as required by law, the App does not integrate any other third-party SDK that collects your information.

## 3. How we use information

We use the local information described in Section 2.1 only to provide app features (for example, to decide whether to show the first-launch agreement dialog). We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not share it with third parties.

## 4. Sharing and disclosure

1. We **do not sell, rent, or share** your personal information with any third party.
2. Disclosure may only be involved in the following limited cases:
   - Transaction-related data is processed by the app store or payment platform under its policies (see 2.4);
   - Where required by laws, regulations, or lawful requests from judicial or administrative authorities.
3. Because we do not operate our own servers that store personal data, there is no transfer of your personal information across borders.

## 5. Storage and retention

1. The information described in Section 2.1 is stored **locally on your device** and retained until you clear the app's data or uninstall the App.
2. After you uninstall the App or clear its data, that local information is deleted. We keep no copy.

## 6. Information security

Because we do not collect or upload your personal information, the relevant risk depends mainly on the security of your own device. We recommend setting a screen lock passcode and keeping your system up to date.

## 7. Children's privacy

1. The App is a sleep-aid tool **intended for adults (parents and caregivers)** and is not designed for children.
2. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you are a guardian and find that a child has used the App without your consent, you may contact us using the details at the end of this Policy.
3. If your region has special requirements regarding children's personal information, please let us know before publishing so that we can make adjustments.

## 8. Your rights

1. Because the relevant information is stored only locally, you can delete it by **clearing the app's data or uninstalling the App**.
2. If you wish to exercise rights such as access, correction, or deletion, or have questions about this Policy, you may contact us using the details below. For information we do not collect, we may be unable to provide additional access or deletion.

## 9. Updates to this Policy

1. We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published in the App or on the relevant page, together with a new "Effective date".
2. For material changes, we will provide a notice in the App. **If you continue to use the App after learning of the update, you are deemed to accept the updated Policy.**
